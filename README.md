# H2 Overview
> Stranger Things is a science fiction horror series created by the Duffer Brothers and released on Netflix. It is set in the 1980s and revolves around a group of kids who encounter supernatural forces and secret government experiments in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana.

# H2 Main Character
1. Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown) – A girl with psychokinetic powers.
2. Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard) – Loyal and caring leader of the group.
3. Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo) - The witty and optimistic friend.
4. Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin) – Brave and resourceful.
5. Will Byers (Noah Schnapp) – The boy who mysteriously disappears in Season 1.

[More info] (https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4574334/?ref_=mv_close)

# H2 Why I love it?
> I love Stranger Things because of its mix of nostalgia, suspense, and heartwarming friendship moments. The 80s vibe, soundtrack, and characters make it an unforgettable experience.
